FAQ

Questions couples ask us

What isn't included in the package price?

Flights aren't included in the package and are booked separately. The package covers group transfers from the airport nearest the hotel and back, accommodation, meals, design, vendors and our production team, as listed under "What the price includes".

Can we change the menu, design or drinks?

Yes. The package is a starting point, and you can change meals, design, drinks and other parts of it. Changes come with an extra charge, and we'll give you the updated price before anything is confirmed.

Can we mix dishes from the two menus?

Yes. The caterer's two recommended menus are flexible: you can swap dishes between them or ask for dishes that aren't on either menu. If a change adds cost, the price is updated and you approve it in advance.

Can we choose our own photographer, makeup artist or DJ?

Yes. You can choose from our list of recommended local vendors or pick a vendor yourself. Either way, our production team coordinates with them and acts as their contact throughout the stay.

Is there a makeup trial? Can family members get makeup too?

Some of the makeup artists we work with offer a trial at no extra charge, so it's worth asking when you choose one. Hair and makeup for bridesmaids and family members is available for an extra charge.

How do we get our photos?

The photographer is with you from getting ready until the end of the event, up to 10 hours. After the wedding, you receive the edited photos by a download link.

Can the order of the days change?

Yes. The usual order is arrival day, then the wedding day, then departure. Every wedding is personal, so the days can be arranged differently, and you can add a taverna night before or after the wedding.

When do we confirm the final guest count?

The final commitment on the number of guests is made up to 3 months before the event. Changes to the group or the room allocation change the final price, so it's best to update us as early as possible.

When is our date reserved?

Our availability and the vendors' is secured only once the work order is signed and the deposit is paid. Until then the date isn't held, so if you have a date in mind, it's worth moving quickly.

How do payments work?

All payments are made in euros, by bank transfer and/or cash, as we agree together. The deposit amount and payment schedule are set together when you sign the work order.

How long is a quote valid?

A quote and the prices in it are valid for 14 days. After that, we'll send you an updated quote.
